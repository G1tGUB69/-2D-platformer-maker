Getting started

  • Pick a tile from the palette at the bottom, paint with the left mouse button, erase with the right mouse button.
  • Make sure the level has a Start tile (and ideally a Goal), then press ▶ Play or Enter.
  • Press Enter or Esc to go back to the editor.
  • Click Save (browser) or Export (file) to keep your level.

Tile palette

Select a tile with its key or from the palette. Middle-click a tile in the level to pick it up as the current tool.

One-way tiles (Platform, Falling, Moving, Spring) can be jumped through from below and landed on from above. The left and right edges of the level act as walls.

Moving platforms

When the Moving tool (M) is selected, the Moving platform panel opens on the left. Its settings apply to the next tiles you paint.

SettingDescription
Path↔ Horizontal · ↕ Vertical · ⤢ Diagonal · ⟳ Circle
DirectionWhich way it starts moving (→ ←, ↑ ↓, ↗ ↘ ↙ ↖, ↻ ↺).
Distance / Radius1 – 20 tiles: length of the trip, or the circle's radius.
Speed0.5 – 15 tiles/s.
  • The tile you place is the start point (A). Straight paths go back and forth between A and B. Circles start at the top, with the centre Radius tiles below.
  • A dashed line and a faded copy show the path in the editor.
  • Paint over an existing moving platform to give it the current settings; middle-click one to copy its settings.
  • Neighbouring tiles with the same settings move together, so you can build wider platforms.

Character settings

Open with 🏃 Character or C. Changes apply immediately, even while playing. While the panel is open, dashed jump arcs are drawn from the Start tile so you can check what the player can reach (orange = standing jump, shown when Speed impact is above 0).

The panel has two collapsible categories:

📏 Metrics

✨ Perks

Abilities the character always has. Each perk's settings appear once it is ticked. Power-ups can still grant a perk for a while even when it is off here.

Reset defaults restores the default values.

Power-ups

Open with ⭐ Power-ups or U. Power-ups are made by you and saved with the level. Five examples are created the first time: Giant, Tiny, Wings (double jump), Feather (glide) and Fire (bouncing projectiles).

Managing power-ups

  • Click a power-up in the list to edit it and select it for painting.
  • ＋ New creates one (up to 25). Duplicate copies the selected one. 🗑 Delete removes it and every copy placed in the level.
  • Name (16 characters max): its first letter is drawn on the pick-up orb. Colour: used for the orb, the player's headband and effects.

Settings

Abilities (tick to enable)

Rules

  • Picking up a power-up replaces the current one.
  • Power-ups are lost when you lose a life; pick-ups come back with the level.
  • A timed power-up's headband blinks during its last 2 seconds.
  • Editing a power-up while playing updates it live.

Enemies

Open with 👾 Enemies or E. Enemy types are made by you and saved with the level, like power-ups. A starter set is created the first time: Walker, Brute (big, 3 HP), Sentry (still), Spiky (can't be stomped), Hoverer (A ↔ B), Swooper (diagonal), Dart (endless), Turret (shoots left and right) and Bomber (drops shots).

  • Click an enemy in the list to edit it and select it for painting. ＋ New, Duplicate and 🗑 Delete work like for power-ups (delete also removes every copy placed in the level).
  • The enemy stands on the tile you paint it on, centred, whatever its size. A–B paths and endless directions are previewed in the editor.
  • Changes apply the next time the level starts (Play, a lost life or R).

ModeBehaviour

⇄ A ↔ BFlies back and forth between its tile (A) and B, Distance tiles away horizontally, vertically or diagonally, through walls.

Combat

  • Health points: each stomp or player shot removes 1 HP. A damaged enemy flashes and shows a health bar; at 0 HP it is defeated and gives its Score when defeated.
  • 🥾 Can be stomped (on by default). Turn it off for an enemy you can't jump on (drawn with spikes): landing on it hurts the player like touching its side, so it must be shot.
  • 🔫 Fires projectiles: while on screen, fires every Time between shots seconds, horizontally (→ Facing, ↔ Both sides) or vertically (↑ Up, ↓ Down, ↕ Up & down). Projectiles fly straight, stop at solid tiles and cost the player 1 HP. Cannons on the enemy show where it fires.

Screen & level size

Screen size

  • Fit window (default) fills the available space.
  • Fixed resolutions (16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 21:9) are letterboxed and scaled down if the window is too small.
  • At most 18 rows are visible at once; taller levels scroll vertically.

Gameplay rules

  • A run lasts from pressing Play until game over. Score and lives carry over between lives; each life restarts the level from the Start tile with full HP.
  • Getting hit (spikes, enemies) costs 1 HP, knocks you back and makes you invulnerable (blinking) for 1.2 s. At 0 HP you lose a life.
  • Pits: falling out of the level always costs a whole life, whatever your HP.
  • Stomping an enemy removes 1 of its HP and bounces you up; hold jump for a full-height bounce. Enemies that can't be stomped hurt you instead.
  • Shots fly straight, stop at solid tiles, remove 1 HP from the enemy they hit and disappear off screen. Enemy projectiles cost you 1 HP.
  • Jump feel: releasing jump early cuts the jump short. A short grace period lets you jump just after leaving a ledge or just before landing.
  • Breakable blocks and Falling platforms come back on each new life.
  • Goal: touching the flag completes the level. R plays again, Enter goes back to editing.
  • HUD (top-left): hearts for HP, then score, coins collected / total, and lives.

Saving, loading & files

  • The browser remembers automatically: character, game-system, power-up and moving-platform settings, screen size and cheats.
  • The level itself is only stored when you click Save (browser) or Export (file). The level saved in the browser loads on start-up.
  • Saved and exported levels include tiles, character, game system, moving platforms and power-ups, so a level file is self-contained. Loading or importing a level replaces your current settings with the level's.
  • Export often: browser storage can be cleared.
