H / F1 / Esc to close · the game is paused

Getting started

  • Pick a tile from the Level elements bar, paint with the left mouse button, erase with the right mouse button.
  • Make sure the level has a Start tile (and ideally a Goal), then press ▶ Play or Enter.
  • Press Enter or Esc to go back to the editor.
  • Click Save (browser) or Export (file) to keep your level.

Tile palette

Select a tile with its key or from the Level elements bar: click a category to select its last-used tile; categories with a ▲ open a list of all their tiles. Middle-click a tile in the level to pick it up as the current tool (this also copies a moving platform's, spring's, flame jet's, bubble spawner's, goal's, text box's, NPC's or merchant's settings).

One-way tiles (Platform, Falling, Moving, upward Spring) can be jumped through from below and landed on from above. The left and right edges of the level act as walls.

Bricks: Power, Coin, Spin and Breakable bricks come back on each new life. If there is no room above a Power brick, its power-up is given to you straight away.

Coins: the value and size of Coin, Big coin and Mega coin are set in ⚙ Game system › 🪙 Coins. Bigger coins are also easier to grab.

Hidden items in Breakable blocks

  • Paint a coin (any kind) or a power-up onto an existing Breakable block to hide it inside. The block shows a hint of its content in the editor.
  • Breaking the block from below collects a hidden coin at once at its full value (it counts towards the coin total); a hidden power-up appears in the block's place.
  • Painting Breakable over the block keeps its content; erasing the block removes it. Hidden items come back on each new life.

Goal pole

When the Goal tool (9) is selected, the Goal panel opens on the left. Pole height: 1 – 20 tiles (default 5); the tile you place is the base.

  • Touching the pole anywhere completes the level.
  • The score depends on how high the character's feet are, from Flag bottom points to Flag top points (⚙ Game system › 🏆 Score). Flag score steps above 1 split the pole into score bands. Both values are shown next to the pole.
  • Paint over a goal to apply the current height; middle-click one to copy it.

NPCs & text boxes

The Text box is in the Terrain category; NPC and Merchant are in the NPCs category. Each one has its own settings, edited in a panel on the left when its tool is selected. As with springs, the panel's settings apply to the next ones you paint: paint over a placed one to apply them, middle-click one to copy its settings. While the tool is selected, every placed one shows its text in a bubble in the editor.

📜 Text box

  • A solid block holding a text of up to 200 characters (line breaks are kept).
  • In play, the text pops up in a bubble above the block while the character touches it (standing on it, pushing against it), and for 3 s after hitting it from below.

🧑 NPC

  • Settings: name, colour and 1 to 5 sentences (empty ones are skipped).
  • In play, stand next to the NPC (a ↓ Talk bubble appears) and press ↓ / S. The game pauses; each press of Space / ↓ shows the next sentence, Esc stops talking.
  • NPCs can't be hurt and don't block the way.

Hazards

Besides Spikes, the Hazards category holds a Flame jet and three zones. Zones fill whole tiles: paint several side by side to make a pool or a cloud; the top row gets a surface.

HazardEffect
🔥 Flame jetSolid burner you can stand on. On a cycle, it shoots a flame straight up; touching the flame costs 1 HP. The burner flickers just before it fires. Flames stop at solid tiles.
🌋 LavaTouching it costs a whole life, like falling into a pit. With invincibility on you are put back on the last solid ground.
💧 WaterSwim: you move at 60 % speed, sink slowly and can press jump at any time to swim up. With your head at the surface, jump leaps out of the water. Air jumps refill in water; gliding and the rotating jump stop.
☠️ PoisonToxic gas you can move through, but it costs 1 HP every Poison: seconds per HP (⚙ Game system › Hazards, 1 s by default); the first hit comes after half that time. Blinking after a hit doesn't protect you; invincibility (cheat or ⭐ power-up) does.

Character settings

Open with 🏃 Character or C. Changes apply immediately, even while playing. While the panel is open, dashed jump arcs are drawn from the Start tile so you can check what the player can reach (orange = standing jump, shown when Speed impact is above 0).

The panel has two collapsible categories:

📏 Metrics

✨ Perks

Abilities the character always has. Each perk's settings appear once it is ticked. Power-ups can still grant a perk for a while even when it is off here.

Reset defaults restores the default values.

Power-ups

Open with ⭐ Power-ups or U. Power-ups are made by you and saved with the level. Eight examples are created the first time: Giant, Tiny, Wings (double jump), Feather (glide), Twister (rotating jump), Climber (wall jump), Fire (bouncing projectiles) and Star (10 s of invincibility).

Managing power-ups

  • Click a power-up in the list to edit it and select it for painting.
  • ＋ New creates one (up to 25). Duplicate copies the selected one. 🗑 Delete removes it and every copy placed in the level (including copies hidden in Breakable blocks).
  • Name (16 characters max): its first letter is drawn on the pick-up orb. Colour: used for the orb, the player's headband and effects.

Settings

Abilities (tick to enable)

Rules

  • Picking up a power-up replaces the current one.
  • Power-ups are lost when you lose a life; pick-ups come back with the level.
  • A timed power-up's headband blinks during its last 2 seconds.
  • Editing a power-up while playing updates it live.
  • When both the character's perks and the power-up have glide, wall jump or rotating jump, the power-up's setting is used.
  • ⭐ Invincibility shows as a rainbow outline. Unlike the Invincibility cheat, it doesn't save you from pits or lava.

Enemies

Open with 👾 Enemies or E. Enemy types are made by you and saved with the level, like power-ups. A starter set is created the first time: Walker, Brute (big, 3 HP), Sentry (still), Spiky (can't be stomped), Helmet (immune to the rotating jump), Hoverer (A ↔ B), Swooper (diagonal), Dart (endless), Turret (shoots left and right) and Bomber (drops shots).

  • Click an enemy in the list to edit it and select it for painting. ＋ New, Duplicate and 🗑 Delete work like for power-ups (delete also removes every copy placed in the level).
  • The enemy stands on the tile you paint it on, centred, whatever its size. A–B paths and endless directions are previewed in the editor.
  • Changes apply the next time the level starts (Play, a lost life or R).

ModeBehaviour
■ StillDoesn't move (floats where it is placed). Facing sets where it looks and fires.

Combat

  • Health points: each stomp or player shot removes 1 HP. A damaged enemy flashes and shows a health bar; at 0 HP it is defeated and gives its Score when defeated.
  • 🥾 Can be stomped (on by default). Turn it off for an enemy you can't jump on (drawn with spikes): landing on it hurts the player like touching its side, so it must be shot.
  • 🌀 Immune to rotating jump (off by default). The character's rotating jump doesn't damage it (drawn with a metal helmet): spinning into it hurts the player instead. Stop spinning (land first) to stomp it, or shoot it.
  • 🔫 Fires projectiles: while on screen, fires every Time between shots seconds, horizontally (→ Facing, ↔ Both sides) or vertically (↑ Up, ↓ Down, ↕ Up & down). Projectiles fly straight, stop at solid tiles and cost the player 1 HP. Cannons on the enemy show where it fires.

Game system (score & lives)

Open the ⚙ Game system menu. It has five collapsible sections: 🏆 Score (coin points and goal-pole points), 🪙 Coins (value and size of each coin kind), ❤️ Lives, ⏱ Time (time limit and the Merchants' price per second) and ⚠️ Hazards. Points per enemy are set per enemy type; health points, starting lives and the Merchants' life / HP prices are in the Character panel.

Time limit: 0 (default) = no timer. Otherwise each life starts with that many seconds, shown in the HUD (red in the last 10 s); running out costs a life. The timer stops while you talk to an NPC or shop, and with the ⏳ Endless time cheat.

Gameplay rules

  • A run lasts from pressing Play until game over. Score and lives carry over between lives; each life restarts the level from the Start tile with full HP.
  • Getting hit (spikes, flames, enemies, enemy projectiles) costs 1 HP, knocks you back and makes you invulnerable (blinking) for 1.2 s. At 0 HP you lose a life.
  • Pits and lava: falling out of the level or touching lava always costs a whole life, whatever your HP.
  • Stomping an enemy removes 1 of its HP and bounces you up; hold jump for a full-height bounce. Enemies that can't be stomped hurt you instead.
  • Shots fly straight, stop at solid tiles, remove 1 HP from the enemy they hit and disappear off screen. Enemy projectiles cost you 1 HP.
  • Jump feel: releasing jump early cuts the jump short. A short grace period lets you jump just after leaving a ledge (or a wall) or just before landing.
  • Rotating jump: every jump spins you until you land (gliding, wall-sliding or getting hit also stops it). While spinning, touching an enemy from any side — even one that can't be stomped — removes 1 of its HP and bounces you off.
  • Invincibility from a ⭐ power-up (rainbow outline) protects you from spikes, flames, poison, enemies and their shots, and defeats any enemy you touch. Pits and lava still cost a life.
  • Wall jump kicks you away from the wall in a full-size jump; you briefly can't steer back toward it.
  • Bricks, coins, power-ups and Falling platforms come back on each new life.
  • Goal: touching the pole completes the level and gives flag points by height (shown on the end screen). R plays again, Enter goes back to editing.
  • Time limit (if set in ⚙ Game system): running out of time costs a life; each life starts with the full time.
  • Coins collected during a life can be spent at Merchants; the counter restarts at 0 on each new life.
  • HUD (top-left): hearts for HP, then score, coins collected / total, lives and the timer (when there is a time limit).

Saving, loading & files

  • The browser remembers automatically: character, game-system, power-up, enemy, moving-platform, spring, flame-jet, bubble, goal, text-box, NPC and merchant settings, screen size, editor layout, cheats and which panel sections are open.
  • The level itself is only stored when you click Save (browser) or Export (file). The level saved in the browser loads on start-up.
  • Saved and exported levels include tiles, character, game system, moving platforms, springs, flame jets, bubble spawners, goals, text boxes, NPCs, merchants, hidden items, power-ups and enemies, so a level file is self-contained. Loading or importing a level replaces your current settings with the level's. Older levels without enemy types still load: their enemies become a Walker.
  • Export often: browser storage can be cleared.

The full file format is described in README.md.
